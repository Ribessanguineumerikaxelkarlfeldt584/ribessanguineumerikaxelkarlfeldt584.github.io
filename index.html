🔍 What Is HeadlessGuard?

HeadlessGuard is a free, open-source macOS app that lives in your menu bar. It automatically detects headless browser processes (like Chromium, Headless Chrome, or those launched by Playwright and Puppeteer) that have been left running after your automation scripts or tests finish. These orphaned processes can eat up memory, slow down your Mac, and even cause privacy issues by keeping background activity alive. HeadlessGuard finds them and lets you stop them with one click — all while leaving your regular Chrome browser completely untouched.

✨ Key Features

  • 🧠 Intelligent Detection: Scans for headless browser processes without interfering with your normal Chrome or Safari windows.
  • 🖥️ Menu Bar App: Sits quietly in your macOS menu bar — always accessible, never in your way.
  • ⚡ One-Click Cleanup: View all orphaned headless browsers and terminate them instantly.
  • 🔒 Privacy First: No data leaves your Mac. All processing is done locally.
  • 🍏 Apple Silicon & Intel: Optimized for both M-series and Intel Macs.
  • 🧩 Developer Tool Friendly: Works perfectly with Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium, and any headless browser automation.

❓ How to Use

1. Launch HeadlessGuard from your Applications folder or Downloads.

2. Look for the shield icon 🛡️ in your macOS menu bar (top right of your screen).

3. Click the icon to see a list of any orphaned headless browsers currently running.

4. Click "Stop All" or select individual processes to terminate them.

5. That's it! Your Mac will feel faster and cleaner instantly.

💡 Why You Need It

If you're a developer, tester, or anyone who uses browser automation tools like Playwright, Puppeteer, or Selenium, you've likely experienced the frustration of headless browser processes lingering after a crash or failed test. They run invisibly, draining your Mac's battery and CPU. HeadlessGuard gives you a simple, safe way to clean them up without risking your open work or personal browsing.

Even if you don't write code, if you use software that spawns headless browsers (such as some web scraping tools or testing utilities), HeadlessGuard can help keep your system running smoothly.

🔧 System Requirements

  • Operating System: macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later (recommended macOS 12+).
  • Architecture: Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) or Intel Mac.
  • Memory: 50 MB of RAM (minimal footprint).
  • Disk Space: Less than 10 MB.
  • Note: This app is designed for macOS only. It does not run on Windows or Linux.

🔒 Privacy & Security

HeadlessGuard runs entirely on your machine. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data. The app only monitors processes that match the headless browser patterns you choose. Your normal Chrome browsing, passwords, and history are never accessed.
